# HealthQueue DORA Metrics Analysis

> **All deployment data here is SYNTHETIC.** It was generated by `dora_metrics.py` (90 days, seed 365) to model a small team with automated CI but manual approval and manual deploy steps. It does not describe real deployments.

Reproduce it with `python dora_metrics.py`. Add `--json dora_report.json --csv dora_deploys.csv` to save the outputs; both file patterns are git-ignored.

## Results (90 days, SYNTHETIC)

| DORA metric | How it's calculated | Result | DORA 2024 level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Deployment frequency** | Production deployments ÷ days | 43 deployments: **0.48/day (3.3/week)**, on 39% of days | **High** (between once per day and once per week) |
| **Lead time for changes** | Median of (deploy time − commit time), per commit | **Median 36.5 h**, p90 3.1 days | **High** (1 day – 1 week) |
| **Change failure rate** | Deployments needing immediate intervention ÷ all deployments | 4 ÷ 43 = **9.3%** | Closest to the **medium** cluster value (10%) |
| **Failed deployment recovery time** | Median time from a failed deployment to service restored | **Median 1.9 h**, p90 16.2 h | **High/Medium** (< 1 day) |

**Where lead time goes** (medians for planned deployments): code review **8.3 h**, automated CI **11 min**, **manual approval wait 11.0 h**, **manual deploy steps 32 min**.

## Benchmarks used

The comparison uses the four performance clusters from the **2024 Accelerate State of DevOps (DORA) report**:

| Level | Deploy frequency | Lead time | Change failure rate | Recovery time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Elite | On demand (multiple/day) | < 1 day | 5% | < 1 hour |
| High | Daily – weekly | 1 day – 1 week | 20% | < 1 day |
| Medium | Weekly – monthly | 1 week – 1 month | 10% | < 1 day |
| Low | Monthly – every 6 months | 1 – 6 months | 40% | 1 week – 1 month |

**Caveats:**
- **The clusters aren't a strict ladder.** In 2024 the *high* cluster's change failure rate (20%) was worse than *medium's* (10%), so the script reports the **closest** cluster for that metric instead of a threshold.
- **The definitions have changed.** In 2023 DORA renamed MTTR to *failed deployment recovery time*, which now covers only failures caused by a change. In 2024 it added a fifth metric, *deployment rework rate* (dora.dev). The script calculates the four metrics this project asked for. Rework rate would be the share of deployments that are unplanned hotfixes, which is **1 of 43 here**.
- **Later reports moved away from four tiers** (to team archetypes). The 2024 table is still widely used as a practical target-setting reference.
- **Small sample.** Only 4 failures in 90 days means the change failure rate and recovery time have wide uncertainty. One more failure would move the rate from 9.3% to about 11.4%. Track trends over several quarters rather than reacting to a single number.

Sources: [dora.dev — DORA metrics](https://dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics/), [dora.dev — history of DORA's metrics](https://dora.dev/insights/dora-metrics-history/), and summaries of the 2024 report's clusters from [Octopus Deploy](https://octopus.com/blog/2024-devops-performance-clusters) and [Coralogix](https://coralogix.com/guides/dora-metrics/). For exact figures, check against the original 2024 report from Google Cloud.

## Analysis

1. **The bottleneck is people waiting, not machines.** CI takes 11 minutes, but the median change spends **11 h waiting for manual approval** and **8.3 h in review**. That's about 19 of the ~36 lead-time hours. Speeding up CI would change almost nothing.
2. **Batching hurts stability.** In the model, each deployment bundles 1–6 commits, and failure probability rises with batch size. Slow approvals encourage bigger batches, which raises the failure rate and makes failures harder to diagnose.
3. **Recovery is acceptable on average but has a long tail.** The p90 is 16 h. The median (1.9 h) misses elite (< 1 h) because detection and rollback are manual. The slowest recoveries are the ones that hurt the error budget in `OBSERVABILITY.md`.
4. **Deploying on only 39% of days** is a sign of release ceremonies, not small continuous changes.

## Recommendations: less manual work, better delivery

| # | Change | Manual step removed | Expected effect | Already in this repo? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Replace the manual release approval with PR review + automated gates.** Branch protection on `main` (1 reviewer + required checks: tests, IaC validation, Trivy) is the approval. Argo CD then deploys automatically. | The ~11 h approval wait | Lead time from ~36 h to well under 1 day (**elite**) | ✅ Gates and Argo CD auto-sync exist; turn on branch protection |
| 2 | **GitOps deploys instead of manual steps.** Merging to `main` updates `newTag` and Argo CD syncs. | 32 min of hands-on deploy work per release | Deploys become routine, enabling multiple per day | ✅ Argo CD manifests exist. **To do:** have CI open a PR that bumps `newTag` to `sha-<commit>` (or use Argo CD Image Updater) |
| 3 | **Smaller batches:** 1 PR = 1 small change, merged daily, behind feature flags if unfinished | Coordination of large releases | Lower change failure rate; easier rollbacks | Process change |
| 4 | **Faster review:** a review-within-4-working-hours agreement, PRs under ~200 lines, CODEOWNERS for auto-assignment | Chasing reviewers | Cuts the 8.3 h review wait | To do |
| 5 | **Automated rollback:** Argo Rollouts canary with SLO-based analysis (error rate, p95 latency from `OBSERVABILITY.md`) | Detecting and rolling back by hand | Recovery time under 1 h (**elite**) | To do |
| 6 | **Burn-rate alerts that page with a runbook link** | Noticing failures | Shorter detection, which is part of recovery time | ✅ Designed in `OBSERVABILITY.md` |
| 7 | **Collect DORA data automatically** from GitHub (commit and merge times) and Argo CD (sync history) instead of synthetic data | Spreadsheet reporting | Continuous, trustworthy metrics | To do: replace the generator with an exporter |
| 8 | **Automated dependency updates** that are tested and scanned by CI | Manual patching | Fewer security-driven emergency deployments | ✅ Dependabot |

**Target for the next 90 days (SYNTHETIC goal):**
- At least 1 deployment per working day
- Median lead time under 24 h
- Change failure rate at or below 10%
- Median recovery under 1 h
